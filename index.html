About completeness

The English set gallery pulls cards classified as holographic from the archived Pokémon TCG card-data repository, plus special Shining / Crystal entries. Promo subsets are separated because a database's “Promo” rarity does not distinguish holo from non-holo versions. Japanese images/rarities come from TCGdex; the archive is not an independently validated exhaustive checklist of every Japanese-exclusive holo printing. Several prize, error, lottery, stadium, and otherwise unnumbered promo printings need specialist references above. “Japanese-only sets” means the set is Japan-exclusive, not necessarily that every card illustration is exclusive or holographic.
